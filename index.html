Dion

What it is
Point-of-sale and wallet platform
My role
Founder and only engineer
When
2026 to now

Dion runs the daily operations of a live gaming lounge: cashier screens, customer wallets, sessions and payments. I designed and built all of it, and I'm the only engineer keeping it running.

The part owners care about most is the remote console. An owner who lives abroad can open it and see sales, sessions, staff activity and the cash position as they happen.

  • 1,500 transactions a month, roughly
  • $20,000 monthly volume, roughly

Built with React, Vite, Node.js and PostgreSQL on AWS.

Post-quantum attendance

What it is
Secure attendance platform for NUST
My role
Internship at NUST, Pakistan
When
Summer 2026

A university attendance system built to stay trustworthy against future quantum computers. I designed and built it end to end during an internship at NUST in Pakistan, then demonstrated the working system to the supervising faculty member.

Students check in by scanning a QR code that rotates and works only once. Every record is signed and chained to the one before it, so any later edit shows up.

  • 539 automated tests
  • 7,063 NIST test vectors

Built with React, Fastify, TypeScript and PostgreSQL 16. This demo fingerprints records with SHA-256 in your browser. The real system uses SHA3-256 and ML-DSA-65 signatures.
